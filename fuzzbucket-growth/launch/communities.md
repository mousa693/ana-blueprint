Prepared 2026-10-21. Nothing has been joined, posted or sent.

## How each link was checked
- A scripted HTTP check ran on 2026-10-21. `200` means the page loaded.
- Facebook group URLs redirect to a login wall, so they can't be verified from here. They're flagged for someone to confirm by hand.
- Member counts come from the page itself where it showed one. Otherwise there's no count.
- All emails listed are published on the organisation's own site.

## Rules of engagement
- Join with a founder's personal profile.
- Read the rules and introduce yourself in the intro channel.
- Contribute for 2+ weeks before mentioning Fuzzbucket.
- Use promo channels (#self-promo, #launches, #tools) only where they exist.
- Always disclose ("I work on Fuzzbucket").
- **Accuracy:** don't call Fuzzbucket free (plans from $19/mo). Publishing is Facebook, Instagram and Threads only.

## C. Pitch copy

### C1. Editorial feature pitch (no payment; for ICYMI, Link in Bio, Geekout, Social Media Today)

> **Subject:** An AI tool that remembers each brand (agency angle, launching 17 Nov)
>
> Hi [Name],
>
> I read [specific recent issue/piece], especially [specific point]. It's why I'm writing.
>
> We're launching **Fuzzbucket** on Product Hunt on 17 November. It's an AI marketing workspace built around a persistent **Brand Brain**: paste a website and social links, and it keeps that brand's voice, audience, products and goals as memory. It then plans the campaign (goal, KPI, channel lanes), generates on-brand images, video and copy, schedules to Facebook, Instagram and Threads, and feeds Meta analytics back into the next plan.
>
> Why it might interest your readers:
> - It's aimed at **social managers juggling multiple brands**: up to 5 brands on Pro, unlimited on Studio.
> - It has a **remote MCP server**, so marketers can use their Brand Brain inside Claude or ChatGPT. As far as we know, that's new for social tools.
> - We're open about limits: Meta-only publishing today, no team roles yet.
>
> Happy to give you a full-access account to judge it yourself, plus screenshots or a 2-minute demo. No expectation of coverage. If it's not a fit, I'd still love your take on what social managers actually need from AI.
>
> [Name], [Title], Fuzzbucket · fuzzbucket.ai
