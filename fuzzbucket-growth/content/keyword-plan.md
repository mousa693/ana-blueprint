# Fuzzbucket Keyword Plan (Q4 2026)

**Owner:** SEO/content lead
**Last researched:** 21 October 2026
**Blog root:** `https://fuzzbucket.ai/resources/blog/`
**ICP:** Agencies and content creators who already use AI. Priority markets: US, UK, CA, AU, EU (English).

## How to read this plan

**Method and limitations.** We did not have Ahrefs, Semrush or Keyword Planner access for this pass. Difficulty is a judgement call based on what currently ranks on page one (checked with live web searches on 21 Oct 2026):

- **Low (L):** page one is mostly small vendor blogs, directories, forum threads or loosely matched content. A new domain with a focused, well-linked page can realistically compete within 3–6 months.
- **Medium (M):** page one mixes established SaaS blogs (SocialPilot, Buffer, Later, Hootsuite, StoryChief) with review sites (G2, Capterra). Needs strong content and some links.
- **High (H):** page one is dominated by category leaders, G2/Capterra and big media. Only worth targeting as a supporting term.

**Before publishing, run the full list through a paid tool** to confirm volume and KD, and swap out anything with zero volume. Keep the intent and page mapping.

**Intent codes:** I = informational, C = commercial investigation, T = transactional, N = navigational.

**Positioning rule for every page:** stick to what fuzzbucket.ai, /pricing and /docs say. That means Brand Brain built from a website or social link; campaign plans; image, video and audio generation; scheduling and publishing to **Facebook Pages, Instagram accounts and Threads profiles** through Meta; a learning loop that uses Meta analytics snapshots; plans from $19/month. Do not claim LinkedIn, X or TikTok publishing, team roles, approval workflows, white-label or a free plan. None of these appear on the site today.
