# The 30-Day AI Content Calendar for Creators

**Who this is for:** solo creators, coaches, consultants and small brands posting on Instagram, Facebook and Threads.
**How it works:** each week is built around **one core idea**, which you spread across formats and platforms. Every slot has a short **AI brief**: a one-line intent you can give any AI tool that already knows your brand. No long prompts needed.
**Time needed:** about 2–2.5 hours per week (plan 20 min, batch-create 60–90 min, schedule 15 min, review 15 min).

---

## Before Day 1: set up your brand context (one-time, ~30 minutes)

Fill this in once. Paste it into your AI tool's brand or knowledge settings. In Fuzzbucket, import your website or Instagram and review the Brand Profile it proposes.

```markdown
# My Brand Context
- I help: [who] to [result]
- I offer: [products/services/links]
- My voice: [adjective], not [opposite] · [adjective], not [opposite] · [adjective], not [opposite]
- Visual style: [palette, photo vs illustration, mood]
- Content pillars: 1) [teach] 2) [show] 3) [prove] 4) [opinion]
- Never: [topics, words, formats I avoid]
- Spelling: [US/UK/CA/AU]
- Main CTA this month: [newsletter / booking / product / DM keyword]
```

**Your four pillars** (the calendar rotates through them):
- **Teach:** tips, how-tos, frameworks
- **Show:** behind the scenes, process, tools, day-in-the-life
- **Prove:** results, testimonials, case studies (real ones only)
- **Opinion:** takes, myths, predictions

## Platform cheat sheet

| | Instagram | Facebook | Threads |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lead with | The visual | The story | The opinion |
| Caption length | Short–medium | Medium–long | Short |
| Best engagement signal | Saves, shares | Comments | Replies |

## Weekly review template (15 minutes)

```markdown
## Week [N] review
- Top post by saves: … → why?
- Top post by comments/replies: … → why?
- Top post by clicks: … → why?
- Worst performer: … → idea, format or hook?
- One learning to add to my brand context: …
- Keep / change next week: …
```

## AI rules for creators (print this)

1. **AI drafts, you decide.** Every post gets a human read before it's scheduled.
2. **Never let AI invent** stories, results, testimonials or statistics.
3. **Edit for voice.** Cut 20% of the words and add one thing only you would say.
4. **Keep one visual style** for the whole month.
5. **Feed learnings back.** Your brand context should improve every week.
