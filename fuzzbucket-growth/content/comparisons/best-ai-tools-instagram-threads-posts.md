# Best AI Tools for Creating and Scheduling Instagram and Threads Posts (2026)

*Last verified: 21 October 2026, against official pricing pages unless noted. Prices change often, so double-check before you buy.*

Instagram and Threads now usually sit side by side in a creator's or agency's plan. Most tools still split the job in two: one tool **creates** the content and another **schedules** it.

This guide compares six tools that do some or all of both, from the point of view of creators and agencies who want AI-assisted posts on Instagram and Threads with as little copying and pasting as possible.

## How we compared

For each tool we looked at:

1. **AI creation depth:** captions only, or visuals and video too? Does it know your brand?
2. **Instagram + Threads publishing:** both natively supported?
3. **Other channels:** important if you post beyond Meta.
4. **Pricing model:** per channel, per user, per brand, or credits.
5. **Who it's best for.**

## 1. Fuzzbucket: best for brand-aware campaigns on Instagram, Facebook and Threads

*Disclosure: this is our product.*

**What it does:** Fuzzbucket builds a reusable **Brand Brain** from your website and socials, plans campaigns (objective, channels, calendar), generates images, video and audio with a choice of models, and schedules or publishes to Facebook Pages, Instagram accounts and Threads profiles via Meta. A learning loop uses Meta analytics snapshots (reach, saves, clicks, comments) to shape future campaigns. ([Homepage](https://fuzzbucket.ai), [Docs](https://fuzzbucket.ai/docs))

**Pricing:** Starter $19/mo (260 credits, 2 brands), Pro $49/mo (680 credits, 5 brands), Studio $149/mo (2,100 credits, unlimited brands). Annual billing gives two months free. Every plan includes the full platform. ([Pricing](https://fuzzbucket.ai/pricing))

**Strengths**
- The deepest *brand context* in this list: AI starts from your website rather than a blank prompt.
- Full creative production (visuals, video, audio) plus Threads and Instagram publishing in one place.
- An MCP connector for Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Cursor, Copilot and Grok.

**Limitations**
- Meta-only publishing. No LinkedIn, TikTok or YouTube.
- Credit-based. Video uses credits much faster than images.
- No free plan or trial listed.

**Best for:** creators and agencies whose audience is on Instagram, Facebook and Threads and who want the AI to *know the brand*.

## 2. Buffer: best low-cost scheduler with a free plan

**What it does:** a simple, popular scheduler with an AI Assistant for writing and repurposing posts. It supports Bluesky, Facebook, Google Business Profile, Instagram, LinkedIn, Mastodon, Pinterest, Substack, Threads, TikTok, X and YouTube. ([Buffer pricing](https://buffer.com/pricing))

**Pricing (official):** Free plan (up to 3 channels, 10 scheduled posts per channel). Essentials $5/month per channel. Team $10/month per channel (adds approvals, access levels, branded reports). The AI Assistant is included on all plans, and paid plans have a 14-day trial.

**Strengths:** very affordable for a few channels, broad network support, approval workflows on Team.

**Limitations:** the AI is text-focused and doesn't generate on-brand visuals or campaign plans. Per-channel pricing adds up for agencies with many clients.

**Best for:** creators on a budget, and anyone who already has visuals and just needs reliable scheduling.

## 3. Later: best for visual planning across many networks

**What it does:** a visual-first social planner with Caption Writer and AI credits, supporting Instagram, Facebook, Threads, Pinterest, TikTok, LinkedIn, YouTube and Snapchat. ([Later pricing](https://later.com/pricing/))

**Pricing (official, billed yearly):** Starter $18.75/mo (1 social set = 8 profiles, 1 user, 5 AI credits). Growth $37.50/mo (2 sets, 2 users, 50 AI credits). Scale $82.50/mo (6 sets, 4 users, 100 AI credits). There's a 14-day trial.

**Strengths:** strong visual grid planning and a "social set" model that suits multi-platform brands.

**Limitations:** AI credits are modest on lower tiers, and the AI is focused on captions rather than full asset generation.

**Best for:** creators and small teams who plan visually and post widely.

## 4. Ocoya: best for AI copy plus very wide integrations

**What it does:** AI content creation and scheduling with automation runs and 28 integrations, including Instagram, Threads, Facebook, LinkedIn, TikTok, Pinterest, Bluesky, YouTube Shorts and more. ([Ocoya pricing](https://www.ocoya.com/pricing))

**Pricing (official, monthly):** Starter $29 (1 user, 5 profiles, 300 AI credits). Team $79 (5 users, 20 profiles, 1,500 credits). Agency $199 (20 users, 100 profiles, 5,000 credits). There's a 7-day trial with a card required.

**Strengths:** an Agency tier sized for many profiles, and broad integrations including commerce and CMS tools.

**Limitations:** lighter brand-strategy features. Third-party listings show different prices (e.g. [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/232714/Ocoya/pricing/)), so check the official page.

**Best for:** teams that want one tool to cover many channels with AI-assisted copy.

## 5. Predis.ai: best for one-click post generation across networks

**What it does:** generates posts, carousels and videos from text input, with scheduling, competitor analysis and e-commerce integrations. Its pricing page lists Facebook, Instagram, Instagram Stories, TikTok, LinkedIn, YouTube Shorts, Google Business Profile and X. Threads isn't listed there. ([Predis pricing](https://predis.ai/pricing/))

**Pricing (official, promotional):** Core $19/mo (1 brand, no auto-posting), Rise $40/mo (4 brands, auto-posting), Enterprise+ $212/mo. There's a 3-day trial with a card required.

**Strengths:** fast creative generation, competitor analysis, Shopify/WooCommerce integrations.

**Limitations:** auto-posting is only on Rise and above. Confirm Threads support with Predis.

**Best for:** e-commerce brands and creators who want quick, template-style posts. See [Fuzzbucket vs Predis.ai](/resources/blog/fuzzbucket-vs-predis-ai).

## 6. Hootsuite: best for larger teams needing enterprise social management

**What it does:** an established social media management suite with AI features. Its plans page says the Standard plan can "generate posts and images with AI". ([Hootsuite plans](https://www.hootsuite.com/plans))

**Pricing (official, annual billing):** Standard from $99/user/mo, Professional from $199/user/mo, Advanced up to $399/user/mo, Enterprise custom. There's a 14-day trial. (Older third-party reviews quote different tiers and a 30-day trial, e.g. [kompozy](https://kompozy.io/reviews/hootsuite), which reflects a 2026 restructuring.)

**Strengths:** mature platform with deep team, inbox and analytics features.

**Limitations:** the highest per-user cost in this list. The plans page we checked doesn't list Threads, so confirm with Hootsuite. AI tools were historically branded OwlyWriter AI, and the current plans page doesn't use that name.

**Best for:** in-house social teams at larger companies.
