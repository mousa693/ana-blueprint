## Part 1. Affiliate and referral program proposal

### 1.1 Current program (from fuzzbucket.ai/affiliate and /affiliate-terms, read 2026-10-21)

- **Commission:** 20% recurring on eligible subscription payments for up to 12 months after conversion.
- **Attribution window:** 60 days for qualified referrals.
- **Platform:** Rekomi handles tracking, approvals and payouts. Payouts are monthly, with a $5 minimum and a 14-day grace period.
- **Approval:** manual review. Target affiliates are creators, newsletters, podcasts, educators, agencies, freelancers, existing users and reviewers who disclose the relationship.
- **Exclusions:** credit top-ups; subscriptions receiving Startups-program benefits; free access, refunds, chargebacks, fraud and self-referrals.

### 1.3 Proposed structure

**A. Creator and affiliate tiers** (Rekomi supports tiered commissions; confirm with Rekomi)

| Tier | Requirement | Commission | Duration | Extras |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Affiliate | Approved | **30%** recurring (up from 20%) | 12 months | Swipe copy, demo brand, UTM builder |
| Pro Partner | 10+ active paid referrals | **35%** | 12 months | Comped Pro account; early feature access |
| Elite Partner | 25+ active paid referrals or audience >50k in ICP | **40%** | 12 months | Co-branded landing page, co-webinar, custom code |
| Launch bonus | Conversions 17 Nov–31 Dec 2026 | Base rate +10 points | First 12 months | Recruitment hook for the PH launch |

- Keep the 60-day attribution window. It's mid-market against the 30–120 day benchmarks.
- Keep the $5 minimum payout. It's a selling point next to Jasper's reported $100.

**B. Activation bounty (supports the 1,000-signup goal)**

- **Pay $1.50 per referred new account that completes a Brand Brain** with a verified email.
- Cap it at $300 per affiliate per month.
- Pay it only once the affiliate also has at least 1 paid conversion that month, or after a 30-day review, so the bounty can't be farmed.
- Publer's reported $0.25 per signup shows the model exists. Ours is higher because it pays for a completed Brand Brain, not just a signup.

**C. Agency partner track**

- **Who:** agencies that use Fuzzbucket for clients, or that refer client brands to it.
- **Terms:** the same tiers as above, plus an "Agency Partner" badge and a listing on a future partners page.
- **Client offer (product must approve):** 15% off the first 3 months for referred client accounts.
- **Fit note:** no team roles or white-label exist today, so the agency pitch is "run up to 5 brands on Pro or unlimited on Studio". Don't promise seats or white-label.

**D. Customer referral (double-sided, in-app)**

- **Referrer:** gets **+150 bonus credits** when the person they referred makes a first paid month.
- **Referee:** gets **+75 bonus credits** on their first paid month.
- **Cost:** credits cost us inference rather than cash. At roughly 1.3 credits per 1K image, 150 credits is about 110 images, which is generous to the user and cheap for us.
- **Guardrails:** one reward per paying referee; exclude Startups-program accounts, which mirrors the affiliate rules.

### 1.4 Unit economics (12-month payout per paid referral, monthly plans)

| Plan | Annual revenue | 20% (now) | 30% (proposed) | 35% | 40% |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Starter $19/mo | $228 | $45.60 | $68.40 | $79.80 | $91.20 |
| Pro $49/mo | $588 | $117.60 | $176.40 | $205.80 | $235.20 |
| Studio $149/mo | $1,788 | $357.60 | $536.40 | $625.80 | $715.20 |

**Decision needed from finance:**
1. Confirm the gross margin after inference and credit costs per plan. The 30% base works only if gross margin stays above about 55% after commission.
2. If margin is tight, the fallback is 25% base with tiers at 30% and 35%, plus the launch bonus.

**KPIs:**
- Number of approved affiliates (target 50 by 31 Dec)
- % with at least 1 conversion
- Brand Brains created via affiliates
- Paid conversions
- Refund and chargeback rate
- Effective CAC compared with paid newsletter buys

### Sponsored integration brief (for creators who say yes to paid)

- **Core message:** "Your AI marketing team that remembers each brand."
- **Must show:**
  - pasting a URL and the Brand Brain result
  - a campaign plan
  - generating 1 image + 1 caption set + 1 short video
  - scheduling to Instagram or Threads
- **Must say:** plans start at $19/mo; publishing goes to Facebook, Instagram and Threads.
- **Must not say:** "free"; LinkedIn, X or TikTok publishing; team or white-label features; any performance stat we haven't verified.
- **CTA:** a tracked link with UTM and creator code.
- **Deliverable approval:** we review a draft for factual accuracy only, within 48h. Creative is the creator's call.
