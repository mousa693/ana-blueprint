## 2. Maker's first comment (post it at 12:01 a.m. PT)

> Hey Product Hunt 👋 I'm [Name], one of the makers of Fuzzbucket.
>
> **Why we built it.** Every agency and creator we talked to ran the same messy stack: ChatGPT for captions, an image model in another tab, Canva to fix the brand colors, a scheduler, and a spreadsheet to remember what worked. Worst of all, none of those tools remembered *the brand*. Every prompt started from zero.
>
> **What Fuzzbucket does:**
> 1. **Brand Brain.** Paste a website and social links. Fuzzbucket decodes the voice, audience, products and goals, and keeps them as a reusable memory for that brand.
> 2. **Strategy map.** Pick a goal and you get a campaign plan: KPI, channel lanes, core message, pillars.
> 3. **Creative studio.** A canvas where concepts, copy variants, images, video and voice are generated with leading models and stay on-brand. Everything stays editable.
> 4. **Publish + learn.** Schedule to Facebook Pages, Instagram and Threads. Meta analytics (reach, saves, clicks, comments) feed back into the next campaign.
>
> **Who it's for:** agencies and freelancers juggling several client brands (Pro covers 5 brands; Studio is unlimited) and creators who want a week of on-brand content without prompt engineering.
>
> **For the AI-native crowd:** there's a remote MCP server (`api.fuzzbucket.ai/mcp`), so you can drive your Brand Brain and generations from Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor and others.
>
> **What it's NOT:** a replacement for your designer or agency. It handles the messy first 80% (briefs, drafts, variations) so the humans can do the last 20%.
>
> Plans start at $19/mo. Today we'd love blunt feedback on two things: (a) how close the Brand Brain gets to your real brand voice on the first pass, and (b) which channel we should support next after Meta.
>
> I'll be here all day answering every comment. 🙏

## 4. Launch-day timeline (all times PT; put a UK/EU owner on the overnight shift)

### T–28 to T–7 days (from 20 October)
- **Ship the PH "coming soon" / upcoming page.** Collect followers; that list gets notified on launch day.
- **Warm up.** Every maker account should be active on PH for at least 2 weeks before launch: comment genuinely on 3–5 launches a day. Brand-new accounts' upvotes count for less.
- **Recruit 40–60 "launch friends"** (Discord members, beta users, agency contacts). Ask them to *check it out and leave honest feedback*. **Never ask for upvotes** in public or in DMs. PH penalises vote-begging and vote rings.
- **Submit directories** on P1 timing (see directories.md) so listings can go live on launch day.
- **Pre-write content:** the X thread, LinkedIn post, IG/Threads carousel (scheduled through Fuzzbucket itself, which is good dogfooding), the Discord announcement, the r/FuzzbucketAI post, and the newsletter email.
- **Optional hunter outreach** (section 5). Self-hunting is fully normal now; a hunter adds reach only if they're active in AI and marketing.

### T–1 day (Monday, 16 November)
- **Schedule the launch** in PH for 12:01 a.m. PT on 17 November. Re-read every field against the guardrails.
- **Test** the UTM link, the signup flow (email, Google, Apple), onboarding and the pricing page on mobile.
- **Brief support.** Watch Discord and help@ all day. Prepare canned answers on pricing and credits, Meta-only publishing and the roadmap.

## 6. Prepared replies to likely comments

- **"Is there a free plan?"** "Not right now. Plans start at $19/mo (260 credits, 2 brands), and yearly billing saves about 16.7%. Credits cover image, video, voice and copy generation."
- **"Does it post to LinkedIn, X or TikTok?"** "Publishing is Meta today: Facebook Pages, Instagram and Threads. You can plan and generate assets for other channels and download them. Which channel should we add next? We're counting votes in this thread."
- **"How is this different from Canva/Jasper/Buffer?"** "Those are great at one step each. Fuzzbucket keeps a persistent Brand Brain per brand and carries it through strategy → creative → scheduling → learning, so you're not re-explaining the brand in every tool."
- **"Agencies: team seats and white-label?"** "Not yet. Today agencies use Pro (5 brands) or Studio (unlimited brands) under one workspace. Tell us what your client workflow needs."
- **"What models do you use?"** "A full catalog across plans: GPT Image, FLUX, Seedance, Grok Imagine video, ElevenLabs and Seed Speech voice, and others. Credits are shown per generation before you run it."
