# Fuzzbucket: 30-day social calendar (X, LinkedIn, Instagram/Threads)

Prepared 2026-10-21. Nothing has been posted or scheduled.

**Window:** Monday 26 October to Tuesday 24 November 2026. **Product Hunt launch is Day 23 (Tuesday 17 November).**
**Audience:** agencies and freelancers managing several brands, plus content creators using AI. Priority markets are the US, UK, CA, AU and the EU.

## Rules for every post

- **Never call Fuzzbucket "free".** If price comes up, say "plans from $19/mo".
- **Publishing claims are limited to Facebook Pages, Instagram and Threads.** No LinkedIn, X or TikTok publishing claims, and no team roles or white-label.
- **Don't post mockup numbers as results.** If a post needs a number, use a real one from our own account or customers, with permission. Placeholders look like `[[real number]]`.
- **Dogfood the Meta posts.** Schedule every Instagram and Threads post *through Fuzzbucket itself* and say so occasionally ("scheduled with Fuzzbucket"). X and LinkedIn posts go out natively or through another tool, because Fuzzbucket doesn't publish there.

**Link and UTM per platform** (all point to fuzzbucket.ai):
- X: `?utm_source=x&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=launch26`
- LinkedIn: `?utm_source=linkedin...`
- Instagram: link in bio with `?utm_source=instagram...`
- Threads: `?utm_source=threads...`

**Weekly themes:**
- Week 1: the problem (brand amnesia)
- Week 2: Brand Brain and how it works
- Week 3: agency and creator workflows, and pre-launch
- Week 4: launch week
- Days 29–30: results and what's next

**Posting times** (local to the bulk of the audience):
- X: 8–10 a.m. ET, with a UK repost at 8 a.m. GMT on key days
- LinkedIn: Tuesday–Thursday, 8–9 a.m. ET
- Instagram/Threads: 11 a.m.–1 p.m. ET

**What the abbreviations mean:**
- **X:** a single post or thread (🧵)
- **LI:** a LinkedIn post from a founder's personal profile, which outperforms a company page
- **IG/Th:** an Instagram caption (carousel or reel noted) plus a Threads version
