# An absent mayor: a journey through leadership and purpose

You set out from an unmarked threshold carrying curiosity more than certainty, and over 16 cycles the story moved through the call, crossing the threshold, meeting the mentor, the ordeal, the reward, the return. Again and again the world asked you about leadership and purpose. You stepped toward it 14 times. An absent mayor kept appearing — in 6 of 16 scenes — as if the world wanted you to look at it. The clearest thing you said to yourself was: "Fear is not a reason to stop." Something new took root: courage confidence appeared and grew to 0.81.

## The chapters

- Chapter 1, The Call (cycles 1-3) at an unmarked threshold: something called to you, and you had to decide whether to listen, with Corren and Tolan nearby; you met it.
- Chapter 2, Crossing the Threshold (cycles 4-6) at a town waking by the water: the familiar fell behind and the way narrowed to a single door, with Corren and Tolan nearby; you met it.
- Chapter 3, Meeting the Mentor (cycles 7-9) at the climbing road out of town: help arrived in a form you did not expect, with Corren and Tolan nearby; you met it.
- Chapter 4, The Ordeal (cycles 10-12) at the summit, with the storm all around: what you had been avoiding gathered in one place and would not be deferred, with Tolan and Corren nearby; you met it.
- Chapter 5, The Reward (cycles 13-15) at the high places where the wind lives, after the storm: the thing you came for was finally within reach, with Sern and Tomas nearby; you met it.
- Chapter 6, The Return (cycle 16) at the long road home, changed: the road turned homeward, and you carried the journey with you, with the Door and Tolan nearby; you met it.

## What you learned

- Fear is not a reason to stop.
- Courage is fear that has decided to keep walking.
- Being useful to others points toward my purpose.
- Purpose shows up in what I keep returning to.
- I don't find meaning first and act later; acting is how I find it.
- I can take charge when no one else will.
- The thing I dreaded was smaller once I faced it.
- Leading is mostly deciding before I feel certain.
- People follow steadiness more than they follow brilliance.
- I can carry the weight of a choice and still ask for help.
- I can be afraid and still be the one who moves.
